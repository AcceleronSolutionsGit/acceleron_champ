The CHAMP console

Everything you need to run the programme: the nomination pool you shortlist from, the moderation queue, the analytics, and the settings behind all of it.

Committee members see Moderation, Nominations, Export and Audit Log. Admins additionally see Behaviours, Settings, Employees and Admin Users.

1
 Nominations — where you shortlist
Opens on the Approved pool, the set managers have already stood behind. Filter by quarter, CHAMP behaviour, function or site, or search names, headlines and evidence. Click any row to read the full evidence and the manager’s note. Export CSV gives you everything matching your current filters, evidence included.
Every export and every removal is recorded in the audit log.
Removing a nomination — read this first
A written reason is required and the employee reads it exactly as you typed it.

• It cannot be undone. There is no restore.
• The quarter then closes to that person — they cannot file a replacement.
• Removing an approved nomination overrides their manager’s judgement. The confirmation box tells you when that’s the case.
• The record is not deleted — the evidence stays readable under the Removed tab.

Read the evidence before you remove, not after. What you cannot do here is approve or decline: that stays with the reporting manager, who knows the work.
2
 Moderation
The flag queue catches two gaming patterns automatically: bursts (one person posting a lot very quickly) and reciprocal loops (two people recognising each other back and forth). Flagged recognitions stay visible and are never removed automatically — you either dismiss the flag or remove the recognition with a reason.
3
 Analytics
Participation over time, splits by function and shift, the behaviour breakdown, direction of recognition, concentration, and dark spots — teams, shifts or sites where nothing is happening. Dark spots is usually the most actionable thing on the page.
Use it to find the groups the programme is not reaching yet.
4
 Settings and the directory (admins)
The cap per pair per month, the minimum reason length and the generic-phrase blocklist; flag thresholds; the nomination window and evidence limits; the weekly digest. Changes take effect immediately and are audited.

The directory syncs from DarwinBox nightly. Two things worth checking after any sync: people with no reporting manager (they cannot file at all) and missing or wrong mobile numbers (the WhatsApp bot won’t recognise them).
One note on messaging
CHAMP only messages people inside a conversation they started themselves. Recognition notifications and the weekly digest reach someone only if they’re currently talking to the bot. That’s intentional, so nobody is woken up by a colleague on another shift.
