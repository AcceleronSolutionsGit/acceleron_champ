You’ll be approving nominations

Along with everything in the all-company mail, you have one extra responsibility in CHAMP: approving the quarterly self-nominations your direct reports file.

There is no “manager” role to be granted. The app reads the reporting line straight from DarwinBox. If someone reports to you there, their nomination comes to you automatically — and it follows them if the line changes.

1
 Finding your queue
Sign in at [CONSOLE URL]. An Approvals link appears in the top navigation once you have at least one direct report. A red number on it means something is waiting.
Each card shows the behaviour claimed and the full evidence — never truncated.
That badge is your only alert
Nothing is pushed to WhatsApp and nothing is mailed to you. That’s deliberate — but it does mean the badge is worth a glance when you sign in.
2
 What you are actually approving
You’re being asked whether this piece of work is real and worth putting in front of the R&R committee with your name attached. Two useful questions: does the evidence actually demonstrate the behaviour they picked, and could you verify the number they quoted if someone asked?
3
 Approving or declining
If you decline, a short note is required, and your report reads it word for word. Make it specific enough to act on — “not enough detail” tells them nothing; “give me the changeover times before and after, and who else ran it” tells them exactly what to fix. They can rework it and send it straight back, and a good decline usually produces a much better second attempt.
Approving? The note is optional, and useful when you have context the committee lacks.
Your decision is final
There is no second approval stage, and you cannot change it afterwards. Approved nominations go straight to the R&R committee. Read before you click.
A few edge cases
Your report can keep editing while it’s awaiting you, so you always see their latest version. If they withdraw it, it leaves your queue. If the committee removes a nomination from the pool, you’ll see it marked Removed with their reason.

If someone who reports to you cannot file at all, their DarwinBox record has no manager against it. HR can fix that; nobody can fix it from inside the app.
Everything in the all-company mail applies to you too — spot recognition on WhatsApp is the part of CHAMP that gets used daily, and teams tend to follow their manager’s lead on it.
