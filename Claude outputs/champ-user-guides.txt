MAIL 1 — ALL EMPLOYEES

Subject: CHAMP is live — recognise a colleague, and put your own work forward

Hello everyone,

CHAMP is now running. It does two things: it lets you recognise a colleague
the moment you see them do something good, and once a quarter it lets you put
your own best piece of work forward for the R&R committee.

Everything below takes a couple of minutes to learn. Save this mail.


── 1. RECOGNISING A COLLEAGUE (WHATSAPP) ──────────────────────────────

Save [WHATSAPP NUMBER] as "Acceleron CHAMP" and send it "hi".

The first time, you will be asked to agree to a short data-protection notice
under the DPDP Act, 2023. Tap "I Agree" once and you will not see it again.

Then:

  1. Tap "Give recognition".
  2. Type the person's name, or their employee code. If several people match,
     you get a list to pick from. If too many match, type more of the name.
  3. Pick the CHAMP behaviour they showed: IMPACT, CARING, ENTREPRENEURSHIP,
     CUSTOMER CENTRICITY, INTEGRITY or INNOVATION.
  4. In one line, say what they actually did.

That is it. They get told, and it appears on the CHAMP feed and the plant
board.

Three rules worth knowing before they surprise you:

  • Be specific. "Great job" and "well done" are rejected on purpose. Write at
    least a short sentence about what the person did. The bot will ask you to
    try again if it is too brief.
  • Two per colleague per month. You can recognise the same person twice in a
    calendar month. After that the bot will tell you the cap is reached.
  • You cannot recognise yourself. That is what the quarterly nomination
    below is for.

Other things you can type at any point:

  "my count"   your own running total, received and given
  "cancel"     abandon whatever you were in the middle of
  "hi"         back to the menu


── 2. THE WEB CONSOLE ─────────────────────────────────────────────────

Go to [CONSOLE URL] and sign in with your Acceleron work email. You will get
a six-digit code by mail. There is no password to remember or forget.

Once you are in:

  Live Feed     everything being recognised across the business
  Directory     search anyone, see what they have given and received
  My Nomination your quarterly nomination (below)

The red "Give Recognition" button at the top right does the same thing as the
WhatsApp flow, if you happen to be at a desk.


── 3. YOUR QUARTERLY NOMINATION ───────────────────────────────────────

Once a quarter you can put forward one piece of your own work. Your reporting
manager approves it, and approved nominations go to the R&R committee.

Go to "My Nomination" and:

  1. Check the quarter. Quarters follow our financial year, so Q1 is
     April–June, Q2 is July–September, and so on. The current quarter is
     always open, and the one that just ended stays open for another 30 days
     so you are not writing it up on the last day.
  2. Pick the CHAMP behaviour your work is an example of.
  3. Write a one-line headline. Something like "Cut Line 3 changeover from 42
     to 18 minutes".
  4. Write the evidence, at least 200 characters.

One nomination per person per quarter. Editing replaces what you sent, so you
can keep refining it right up until your manager decides.

What makes the difference between a nomination that lands and one that does
not:

  • Name the change, not the effort. "Reduced rejections on Line 3 from 4.2%
    to 1.1%" beats "worked very hard on quality".
  • Give one number. Time saved, defects avoided, cost, tickets closed,
    uptime, whatever your work is actually measured by.
  • Say who can confirm it. A colleague, a report, a ticket number. This is
    the part that turns a claim into evidence.
  • Match the behaviour you picked. If you claimed INNOVATION, the evidence
    has to be about doing something new, not about working hard on something
    familiar.
  • Stay inside the quarter. Work from earlier quarters belongs to those
    quarters.

Your manager is signing their name to this, so write something they can
check.

After you send it:

  Awaiting your manager   they can see it, you can still edit it
  Approved                locked, and with the R&R committee
  Not approved            read their note, rework it, send it again
  Withdrawn               you pulled it back; edit and resend any time

If your manager does not approve it, that is not the end. You can rework the
evidence and resubmit, and it goes straight back to them.


── 4. THE BOT WILL NOT PESTER YOU ─────────────────────────────────────

CHAMP only messages you inside a conversation you started. If you begin
something and get pulled away, you will get one reminder after five minutes,
and nothing after that. Your unfinished flow stays where you left it for 30
minutes; after that just say "hi" and start again.


── QUESTIONS ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

If the bot says your number is not registered, or the app shows no reporting
manager against you, that is a directory problem, not something you can fix
from the app. Write to [HR CONTACT].

Anything else, [R&R CONTACT].

MAIL 2 — REPORTING MANAGERS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Subject: CHAMP: you will be approving your team's quarterly nominations

Hello,

Along with everything in the all-company mail, you have one extra
responsibility in CHAMP: approving the quarterly self-nominations your direct
reports file.

There is no "manager" role to be granted. The app reads the reporting line
from DarwinBox. If someone reports to you there, their nomination comes to
you, automatically, and it follows them if the line changes.


── FINDING YOUR QUEUE ─────────────────────────────────────────────────

Sign in at [CONSOLE URL] with your work email. An "Approvals" link appears in
the top navigation once you have at least one direct report. A red number on
it means something is waiting.

That badge is the only alert you get. Nothing is pushed to WhatsApp and
nothing is mailed to you, which is deliberate — but it does mean the badge is
worth glancing at when you sign in.

The queue has four tabs: Awaiting you, Approved, Not approved, and
Everything.


── WHAT YOU ARE ACTUALLY APPROVING ────────────────────────────────────

Each card shows you the person, the quarter it covers, the CHAMP behaviour
they claimed, their one-line headline, and the full evidence. Nothing is
truncated, on purpose. You are being asked whether this piece of work is real
and worth putting in front of the R&R committee with your name attached.

Two useful questions: does the evidence actually demonstrate the behaviour
they picked, and could you verify the number they quoted if someone asked?


── APPROVING OR DECLINING ─────────────────────────────────────────────

Approve (navy) or Decline (red).

If you decline, a short note is required, and your report reads it word for
word. Make it specific enough to act on. "Not enough detail" tells them
nothing; "give me the changeover times before and after, and who else ran it"
tells them exactly what to fix. They can rework it and send it back to you,
and a good decline usually produces a much better second attempt.

If you approve, you can leave an optional note for the committee. Worth doing
when you have context they would not otherwise have.

Your decision is final. Approved nominations go straight to the R&R
committee; there is no second approval stage. You can review anything you
have decided under the other tabs, but you cannot change it afterwards, so
read before you click.


── A FEW EDGE CASES ───────────────────────────────────────────────────

  • Your report can keep editing while it is awaiting you, so what you see is
    always their latest version.
  • If they withdraw it, it leaves your queue.
  • If the R&R committee removes a nomination from the pool, you will see it
    marked Removed with their reason.
  • If someone who reports to you cannot file at all, their DarwinBox record
    probably has no manager against it. [HR CONTACT] can fix that; nobody can
    fix it from inside the app.


── AND THE EVERYDAY PART ──────────────────────────────────────────────

Everything in the all-company mail applies to you too. Spot recognition on
WhatsApp is the part of CHAMP that gets used daily, and teams tend to follow
their manager's lead on it.

Subject: CHAMP console: nominations, moderation and programme settings

Hello,

This covers the console. Committee members see Moderation, Nominations,
Export and Audit Log. Admins additionally see Behaviours, Settings, Employees
and Admin Users.

Sign in at [CONSOLE URL] with your work email, then open Console from the top
navigation.


── NOMINATIONS ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

This is where you shortlist. It opens on the Approved pool, which is the set
managers have already stood behind.

Filter by quarter, CHAMP behaviour, function, site, or search names,
headlines and evidence. Click any row to read the full evidence and the
manager's note. Export CSV gives you everything matching your current
filters, including the evidence in full, so you can work through it offline
or circulate it.

Removing a nomination. If an entry does not belong in the pool — a duplicate,
the wrong quarter, something copied from elsewhere, something inappropriate —
you can remove it. A written reason of at least 15 characters is required and
the employee reads it exactly as you typed it.

Be deliberate about this:

  • It cannot be undone. There is no restore.
  • The quarter then closes to that person. They cannot file a replacement.
  • Removing an approved nomination overrides their manager's judgement. The
    confirmation box tells you when that is the case, and the audit log
    records it.
  • The record is not deleted. The evidence stays readable under the Removed
    tab.

A mistaken removal has to be sorted out with HR, so read the evidence before
you remove, not after.

What you cannot do here is approve or decline. That stays with the reporting
manager, who knows the work.


── MODERATION ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The flag queue catches two gaming patterns automatically: bursts (one person
posting a lot of recognitions very quickly) and reciprocal loops (two people
recognising each other back and forth). Flagged recognitions stay visible and
are never removed automatically. You either dismiss the flag or remove the
recognition with a reason.

Below the queue is every recognition across every status, searchable and
filterable, with the same remove-with-reason action.


── ANALYTICS ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Participation over time, splits by function and shift, the behaviour
breakdown, direction of recognition (junior to senior, peer to peer, across
functions), concentration (whether a small group is doing all the
recognising), and dark spots — teams, shifts or sites where nothing is
happening. The dark spots view is usually the most actionable thing on the
page.


── EXPORT AND AUDIT ───────────────────────────────────────────────────

Export gives you filtered recognitions as CSV or Excel, with the full
moderation trail. Every export is audited.

The Audit Log records every sign-in, every moderation action, every
nomination decision and removal, every settings change, and who did it.


── SETTINGS (ADMINS) ──────────────────────────────────────────────────

Recognition rules: the cap per pair per month (currently 2), the minimum
reason length, and the blocklist of generic phrases rejected as a whole
reason.

Flag thresholds: how many recognitions in what window counts as a burst, and
what counts as a loop.

Quarterly self-nomination: switch it on or off entirely, set the minimum and
maximum evidence length, and set the late-filing window — how many days after
a quarter ends people can still file for it. Quarters follow the Indian
financial year, so Q1 is April–June.

Weekly digest: on or off, and whether it goes to everyone or leadership only.

Changing these takes effect immediately and is recorded in the audit log.


── EMPLOYEES AND THE DIRECTORY (ADMINS) ───────────────────────────────

The directory is synced from DarwinBox nightly, and can be synced on demand
from the Settings tab. It is also what decides who approves whose nomination,
through the reporting line.

Two things are worth checking after any sync: people with no reporting
manager against them (they cannot file a nomination at all), and people whose
mobile number is missing or wrong (the WhatsApp bot will not recognise them).

Behaviours can be renamed, re-described, recoloured or retired. Retiring one
removes it from both the recognition flow and the nomination picker
immediately. Existing records keep the behaviour they were filed against.


── ONE NOTE ON MESSAGING ──────────────────────────────────────────────

CHAMP only messages people inside a conversation they started themselves.
Recognition notifications and the weekly digest reach someone only if they
are currently talking to the bot. That is intentional, so nobody is woken up
by a colleague on another shift. If you ever need the old broadcast
behaviour, that is a configuration change, not a console setting — talk to
whoever runs the deployment.

Questions on any of this, [R&R CONTACT].
