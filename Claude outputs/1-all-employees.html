 Recognise a colleague on WhatsApp
Save [WHATSAPP NUMBER] as “Acceleron CHAMP” and send it hi. The first time, tap I Agree on the short data-protection notice — you will only see it once.

The whole thing takes about thirty seconds on your phone.
Three rules worth knowing
Be specific. “Great job” and “well done” are rejected on purpose — write a short sentence about what they did.

Two per colleague per month. After that the bot tells you the cap is reached.

You can’t recognise yourself. That’s what the quarterly nomination is for.

Type my count for your own running total, cancel to abandon whatever you were doing, or hi to get back to the menu.

3
 Put your own work forward, once a quarter
Open My Nomination. Pick the quarter, pick the CHAMP behaviour your work is an example of, write a one-line headline, then the evidence.

Quarters follow our financial year, so Q1 is April–June. The current quarter is always open, and the one that just ended stays open another 30 days — so you’re not writing it up on the last day. One nomination per quarter; editing replaces what you sent.

Your reporting manager approves it. Approved nominations go to the R&R committee.
What makes a nomination land
Name the change, not the effort. “Reduced rejections on Line 3 from 4.2% to 1.1%” beats “worked very hard on quality”.

Give one number. Time saved, defects avoided, cost, tickets closed, uptime.

Say who can confirm it. A colleague, a report, a ticket number. This is the part that turns a claim into evidence.

Match the behaviour you picked, and stay inside the quarter.

Your manager is signing their name to this, so write something they can check.
